Unit 2: How Works Enter the Public Domain

There are only a few doors into the public domain: the term expires, the work was made by the U.S. federal government, the owner dedicates it (for example with CC0), or, for older U.S. works, the owner failed a formality such as notice or renewal.

This module uses sequential progress: you must complete each requirement in order. It unlocks only after you complete Unit 1.
