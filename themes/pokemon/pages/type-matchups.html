Key rules

  • Dual types multiply: Water vs. Rock/Ground (Geodude) = 2 × 2 = 4×.
  • Same-type attack bonus (STAB) multiplies damage by 1.5.
  • In Gen I, Psychic was nearly unstoppable: Ghost moves were bugged and did nothing to it.
