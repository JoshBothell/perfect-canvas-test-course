Welcome to the Pokémon Trainer Academy!

This course follows a trainer's journey through the first three generations of Pokémon: Kanto, Johto, and Hoenn. Each region is one module. Along the way you'll read field guides, file Pokédex entries, battle through quizzes, and debate strategy with your rivals.

For developers: this is a test fixture course. The Pokémon theme is filler. Every item exists to exercise a native Canvas feature. The unpublished page {{ a_page('developer-notes') }} lists what each item tests.
