Welcome to Johto

Johto is the setting of Pokémon Gold, Silver and Crystal (1999–2000). Gen II added 100 species (#152–#251), the Dark and Steel types, held items, breeding, a real-time clock, and shiny Pokémon.

This module uses sequential progress: you must complete each requirement in order. It unlocks only after you complete Kanto.
