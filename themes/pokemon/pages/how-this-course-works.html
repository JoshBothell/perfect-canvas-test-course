How This Course Works

Each region module follows the same rhythm:

  1. 📖 Learn: read the region guide and reference pages.
  2. ✍️ Practice: submit Pokédex entries and field notes (assignments).
  3. 💬 Discuss: debate strategy with other trainers.
  4. ⚔️ Battle: take the Gym quiz. A passing score unlocks the next region.

Late Work

Late submissions lose 10% per day, but never drop below 50% of earned points. Missing work is not automatically penalized.
