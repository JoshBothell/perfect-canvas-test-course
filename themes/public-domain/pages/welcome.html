Welcome to Public Domain Academy!

This course is a tour of the public domain: the huge commons of books, films, music and art that anyone may use, copy and remix without permission. Each unit is one module: Copyright Foundations, How Works Enter the Public Domain, and Using the Public Domain. Along the way you'll read statutes, write status memos, take quizzes on a collection of 386 public-domain artworks, and argue cases with your classmates.

For developers: this is a test fixture course. The public-domain theme is filler. Every item exists to exercise a native Canvas feature. The unpublished page {{ a_page('developer-notes') }} lists what each item tests.
