PDA-101 Syllabus: Public Domain Academy

Instructor: Professor Van Helsing · TA: Ishmael · Office hours: weekly (see Calendar)

Course Description

An introduction to the public domain: what copyright protects, how and when works become free, and how to reuse them responsibly. Examples are drawn from a collection of 386 public-domain artworks made before 1931. Educational test-fixture content, not legal advice.
