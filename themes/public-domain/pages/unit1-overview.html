Unit 1: Copyright Foundations

Before you can say a work is free, you need to know what copyright protects in the first place. This unit covers the Copyright Clause of the U.S. Constitution, what counts as an original work of authorship, the line between ideas and expression, and the basic arithmetic of copyright terms.
