Unit 3: Using the Public Domain

Knowing a work is free is only half the job. This unit covers the edge cases: new material in adaptations is still protected, trademarks follow different rules than copyright (think of Steamboat Willie, free since 2024), terms differ between countries, sound recordings have their own schedule, and Bridgeman v. Corel asks whether a photograph of a public-domain painting is itself protected.
